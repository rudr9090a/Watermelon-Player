1. Overview
   Theme: Cinematic Industrial, Utilitarian, High-Contrast, Technical.
   Purpose: A landing page that conveys extreme reliability, scale, and advanced engineering for underground mining operations.
   Vibe: Gritty yet precise. The intersection of raw earth and high-tech robotics. It avoids the soft, rounded, pastel look of AI-generated SaaS pages in favor of sharp edges, deep shadows, and real-world imagery.

2. Color Palette
   The color scheme is derived from the subterranean environment: deep shadows, cool rock faces, and piercing artificial light.

Role	Color	Hex (Approx.)	Usage
Primary Background	Deep Slate / Black	#0B0E14	Main background, negative space
Primary Text	Pure White	#FFFFFF	Headlines, active UI elements
Secondary Text	Muted Steel	#8C96A5	Subtext, technical data, inactive links
Accent / Action	Industrial Amber	#F5A623	Call-to-action buttons, key data highlights, hover states (inspired by the safety gear/headlights)
UI Borders	Dark Gunmetal	#2A323D	Card outlines, dividers, subtle grid lines
3. Typography
   The typography relies on extreme contrast between massive, clean headlines and tiny, technical data points.

Primary Display (Headlines): Neo-Grotesque Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, Helvetica Now Display, or Neue Haas Grotesk).

Styling: Tight tracking (letter-spacing), heavy weight (Bold/Black), left-aligned. Massive scale (h1 can be 72px - 96px+).

Technical / Data (Microcopy): Monospace (e.g., JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, or Space Mono).

Styling: Uppercase, wide tracking. Used for small labels, metrics (e.g., "+ 1.000.000 LINEAR METERS"), and navigation menus to give a "readout" or "terminal" feel.

Body Copy: Clean Sans-Serif (Inter or Roboto).

Styling: Highly legible, max-width restricted to ensure readability against dark backgrounds.

4. Layout & Structure
   The Cinematic Asymmetry: The page is designed like a film still. It uses edge-to-edge visuals with UI elements anchored to the corners, creating a diagonal visual flow.

Full-Bleed Hero: 100vh height. The background is a dark, high-resolution image or video of heavy machinery in a cavernous environment.

Grid: A hidden 12-column grid. The main headline occupies the left 6-8 columns. Interactive elements are anchored to the bottom right.

Texture: A subtle, global film grain/noise overlay (opacity 5-10%) is applied over the entire site to kill the "digital flatness" and add an organic, photographic feel.

5. Component Breakdown
   Header (Top Edge)
   Logo (Top Left): White, sharp, geometric monogram. No soft rounded edges.

Menu (Top Right): The word "Menu" in Monospace font, paired with a perfect circle outline containing two thick horizontal lines. Hovering over it should invert colors (White circle, dark lines).

Hero Section (The Main Event)
Background: Dark, moody photography. A continuous loop video is preferred (e.g., slow-motion dust settling, headlights moving in the dark).

Headline: Left-aligned. Example: "Your trusted partner in autonomous extraction." (White).

Technical Overlay (Mid-right): Floating over the image, aligned with the light source of the machine in the background. Tiny monospace text: STATUS: ACTIVE / DEPTH: 1,200M / EFFICIENCY: +1,000,000 LINEAR METERS. This adds realism and context.

Interactive Cards (Bottom Right)
Replacing the Cominvi cards with a more refined interaction.

Structure: Two or three overlapping (or side-by-side) rectangular cards.

Styling: Sharp corners (0px border radius) or a very slight 2px radius. A thin #2A323D border.

Imagery: High-contrast, almost black-and-white photography (e.g., a miner looking at a tablet, a close-up of a drill bit).

Bottom Bar: A solid white bar at the bottom of the card containing the link text ("OUR SERVICES", "TECHNOLOGY") in bold, black monospace.

Action Indicator: A sharp, geometric arrow → or ▸ on the far right of the white bar.

Hover State: On hover, the image zooms in slightly (scale 1.05), the white bottom bar changes to the Industrial Amber accent color, and the arrow translates to the right.

"The Anti-AI" Invisible Details
No Center Alignment: Everything is anchored to edges.

Intentional Contrast: The text isn't just placed randomly; it interacts with the lighting of the background image.

Tactile Buttons: If a primary button is needed (e.g., "Request a Consultation"), it should have a sharp 2px radius, solid background, and clear borders. No soft drop shadows or pastel gradients.

6. Imagery Guidelines
   Lens Flares & Light: Use real photographic light. The blueish lens flare in the reference image is key—it bridges the gap between the dark rock and the bright machinery.

Color Grading: Images should be graded with deep blacks (crushed shadows) and cool blues, with warm yellows/oranges reserved only for light sources or safety equipment.

No Stock Smiles: Show the grit of the work. Dust, rock textures, heavy machinery. If people are shown, they should be in full PPE, silhouetted or lit dramatically by machinery.

7. Motion & Interaction
   Initial Load: The background image fades in slowly, followed by a staggered reveal of the headline (sliding up slightly).

Parallax: As the user scrolls down, the background image should move at 50% speed to create depth.

Cursor: Consider a custom cursor (a small white crosshair or a tiny dot) to reinforce the technical, precision-engineering vibe.
